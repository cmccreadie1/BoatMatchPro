BoatMatch Pro - Guide

1. Registration: Add anglers, assign their boat number/name, and team name (if competing in teams of 4).

2. Score Entry: Input Raw Points, Species Count, and Total Fish for each angler grouped by boat.

3. Boat Percentage Calculation: The boat winner receives 100%. Other anglers on the same boat receive a percentage relative to the winner's raw points.

4. Team Rankings: Teams must have exactly 4 members to qualify. Rankings are based on the sum of individual boat percentages.

5. Data Privacy: All data is strictly stored locally in your browser cache.
