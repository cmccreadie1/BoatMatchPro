📖 BoatMatch Pro — User Guide & FAQ

1. Quick Start & Match Setup

  • Tournament Details: Enter your Competition Name and Date on the Setup tab.
  • Boat Generation: Set the total Number of Boats competing. The app will automatically create individual boat cards.
  • Customizing Boats: Rename any boat label (e.g., BOAT 1 - PREDATOR) in the designated Boat Name box.
  • Adding Anglers: Click ➕ Add Angler on any boat card. Anglers are automatically assigned a sequential number (1, 2, 3...) to help you track boat capacity at a glance.
  • Fast Team Entry: As you type a team name, the app remembers and suggests existing team names used in the match to eliminate typos and keep team scoring accurate.

2. Post-Match Score Entry

  • Entering Scorecards: Once paper scorecards are brought back to the desk, navigate to the 📝 Score Entry tab.
  • Entering Raw Data: Enter each angler's Raw Boat Points, Species Count, and Total Fish Count.
  • Live Percentage Preview: As soon as raw points are typed, the app automatically identifies the boat winner and previews relative percentages in real-time.

3. How Scoring & Rankings Work

  • Relative Boat Performance: The boat winner receives 100.00%. Other anglers on that boat are awarded a percentage relative to the boat winner's raw points.
  • Individual Tie-Breakers: Ties on percentage are automatically resolved using:
    1. Percentage Score (%)
    2. Raw Boat Points
    3. Total Species Count
    4. Total Fish Count

4. 4-Person Team Competition

  • Team Qualification Rule: A team must have exactly 4 registered anglers to qualify. Teams with fewer or more members are marked as Ineligible.
  • Team Score Formula: Team standings are calculated by summing the 4 individual member percentage scores together.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ Is my tournament data private and secure?
Yes, 100%. All data entered into this application is stored exclusively on your device (in your browser's local storage). No information, angler names, or scores are ever transmitted to external servers, cloud storage, or third parties.

❓ What happens if I close my browser or turn off my laptop?
Your data is completely safe. Because data saves automatically after every single keystroke, you can safely close the browser tab, close the browser app, restart your device, or lose internet connection completely. When you reopen the web app, all your tournament setup and entered scores will be exactly as you left them.

❓ Does the app work without an internet connection?
Yes. Once loaded for the first time in your web browser, the built-in Progressive Web App (PWA) Service Worker saves all required files directly to your device. You can run entire multi-day competitions offshore or in remote hotel rooms with zero internet or cellular coverage.

❓ How do I clear the app for the next competition day?
When a match is finished and printed, click the red 🔄 New Match button in the top right corner. After confirming, the local memory will be wiped clean and ready for your next event.
