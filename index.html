BoatMatch Pro — User Guide

Quick Start & Match Setup
How to set up your competition, fleet, and angler rosters on your computer or tablet.
STEP 1 Enter Tournament Details
Open the Setup & Registration tab and enter your official Competition Name and Date.
STEP 2 Generate Boats
Type the total Number of Boats competing. The app instantly creates an individual management card for every boat in the fleet.
STEP 3 Customise Boat Names
Rename default boat labels to match actual vessel names (e.g., change BOAT 1 to BOAT 1 - PREDATOR).
STEP 4 Register Anglers & Teams
Click Add Angler on any boat card. Type the angler's name and team name. As you type team names, the app auto-suggests existing teams to prevent spelling mismatches.
💡 Unused / Blank Slots: If a boat has empty angler slots or an angler pulls out, simply leave the slot empty or click Delete. Blank slots are completely ignored by the scoring engine and won't affect calculations.
Entering Scores, Corrections & PDF Exports
How to transcribe paper scorecards, make edits, and print official results.
STEP 1 Switch to Score Entry
When paper scorecards return to the desk, click over to the Score Entry tab.
STEP 2 Enter Paper Card Data
Type each angler's Raw Boat Points, Species Count, and Total Fish Count directly into the 2-column grid.
STEP 3 Real-Time Updates & Corrections
Percentages, leaderboards, and team totals update instantly as you type. If you misread a paper scorecard, simply overtype the numbers—all standings recalculate in real time automatically.
STEP 4 Printing / Exporting PDF Reports
At any point during or after the match, click the Print Leaderboards button in the top header to generate an official print-ready PDF report containing Boat Standings, Individual Leaderboards, and Team Leaderboards.
Scoring Calculations & Team Rules
How relative boat percentages, tie-breakers, and team competitions operate.
Relative Boat Scoring
The top angler on each individual boat receives 100.00%. All other anglers on that boat receive a percentage score proportional to the boat winner's raw points.
Automatic Tie-Breakers
Ties on percentage are automatically resolved in strict order using:
  1. Percentage Score (%)
  2. Raw Boat Points
  3. Total Species Count
  4. Total Fish Count
Team Competition
• All teams with registered members are fully eligible to compete.
• Team standings are calculated by combining the individual percentage scores of all members.
• Smaller teams naturally accept a handicap based on their member count.
Secret Pairs Mini-Comp Rules
How the optional post-match target draw side-pot works.
Random Pairing & Target Score
Anglers who opt into the side-pot are randomly paired post-match. The app picks a secret Target Score strictly between the lowest possible pair sum and highest possible pair sum from the match entries.
Joe Average Virtual Angler
If an odd number of anglers opt in, the app automatically creates a virtual partner named "Joe Average" whose raw points equal the exact rounded average score of all active participants.
Winning & Tie-Breaker
The pair whose combined raw points are closest to the Target Score wins. On an exact distance tie (e.g. +5 vs -5), the pair with the higher combined raw score wins.
Match Safety, Security & Data Management
Clear answers on data privacy, offline protection, and clearing the app.
Can anyone on another computer alter my scores?
No. All match data stays strictly locked inside your laptop/tablet browser storage. Opening the link on another computer loads a completely separate, blank app—there is no shared cloud database or server connection.
What if I accidentally refresh the browser or restart my computer?
Your data is completely safe. Every keystroke saves instantly to local storage. You can close the tab, restart your computer, or lose power without losing a single point.
Does the app work offline without internet signal?
Yes. Once opened in your browser, the built-in Service Worker caches all files onto your device, allowing you to run entire matches and generate PDFs with zero internet connection.
How do I reset the app for the next match day?
When your tournament is complete, click the red Reset / Clear Match button in the top header. After confirming, the app automatically exports a complete PDF report (BoatMatch_Pro_[COMP_NAME]_[DATE].pdf) and clears memory for your next event.
